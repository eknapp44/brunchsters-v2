**Testing notes:**

- **What was tested:** **`cancelBrunch`**: calls off an active brunch and a confirmed one that has not happened (records `statusBeforeCancelId`, `cancelledAt`, `cancelledBy`, clears `cancelNoticeSentAt`, emits `brunch/cancelled` with the ISO time); the order of checks (outsider `brunch_not_found`, non-host member `not_host`, already called off `brunch_called_off`, a draft `invalid_transition`, a confirmed brunch whose time has passed `brunch_over`), and that every refusal before the budget **charges nothing**; the budget is one unit of `cancel-notices-host` keyed by the **host**, never the brunch; a spent day or a failing limiter writes and emits nothing; losing a race to another status change gives `invalid_transition` and emits nothing; a missing status row is `lookup_not_found`; a queue failure does not fail the cancel. **`reinstateBrunch`**: restores `statusBeforeCancelId`, changes **only** the status (the cancel bookkeeping stays for the back-on email), refuses a brunch that is not called off, one with nothing recorded to restore, a restore the table forbids, and loses cleanly to a concurrent change. **`deleteBrunch`**: soft-deletes a draft and a called-off brunch, refuses an active or confirmed one (`not_deletable`), a second delete is a 404, a non-host gets the 404/403 split. **`setBrunchHidden`**: marks the viewer's own attendee row (and only that), clears it, is idempotent, looks only at a live brunch, treats a non-member or a deleted brunch as `brunch_not_found`. **Dashboard**: `groupDashboard` drops hidden brunches out of next-up, the list and Past and returns them on their own (including a person who hid everything); `limitPast` shows five, counts the rest, shows all on request, and keeps the order. `getBrunchesForUser` and `getBrunchById` report the viewer's own `isHidden`. **Real Postgres (10 tests):** calling off then bringing back a confirmed brunch leaves options (with their confirmed flags), votes, invites (tokens, expiry) and answers byte-identical, status goes `confirmed -> cancelled -> confirmed`; an active one comes back active; a second cancel and a second bring-back are refused; a draft and a passed confirmed brunch cannot be called off; every member write is refused while called off and works after; **E11 in both directions, deterministically**: a cancel that read "active" loses to a confirm that commits in between and does not clobber it, and a confirm that read "active" loses to a cancel that commits in between and its confirmed flags are rolled back; delete works for a draft and a called-off brunch, refuses a live one, keeps the row (soft); **a deleted brunch gives the host, a member and a link holder exactly the answers a made-up id gives** across eleven operations plus membership, its link goes cold, and it is on nobody's dashboard; hiding is one person's: the host hides it, the member still sees it, un-hiding returns it, it works on a called-off brunch, and an outsider cannot hide what is not theirs.
