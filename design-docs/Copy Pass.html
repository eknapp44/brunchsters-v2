Five principles I followed

If a rewrite didn't pass at least one of these, I left the line alone.

Verbs over nouns

Plan a brunch becomes Round up the crew. Cancel brunch becomes Call it off. Always favor the action you'd actually say out loud.

Specific beats generic

Tartine is leading by 2 votes becomes Tartine's up by 2. Same info, half the syllables, sounds like a friend texting.

Friend, not service

Your event has been successfully scheduled would never survive. Friends don't say "successfully." They say Brunch on. 💕

No corporate words

Killed every event, attendees, leverage, engage, community, action required. Replaced with brunch, gang, the crew, what's happening.

Trust the user

Removed half the "click here to" and "you can do X" hedges. If the button says Save it, you save it. Don't narrate.

Dashboard

Status pills and primary actions.

Pills should describe a moment in time, not a system state. "Voting open" is a status; "Open for votes" is what a friend would say. "Planning" is generic; "In the works" sounds like someone working on it.

These already sing in the brand voice. Don't break what works.
